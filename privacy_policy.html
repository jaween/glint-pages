Effective Date: 2026-02-28

Flick Fu is operated by Jaween Ediriweera ("we", "us", or "our"), a sole trader registered in Australia.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: jaween.ediriweera@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

Flick Fu is designed to collect minimal personal data. We may collect the following information:

  • Typing speed and performance scores
  • Leaderboard rankings
  • Country (derived from device or IP region)
  • Advertising ID (for ads and analytics)
  • Error and crash reports (via Sentry)
  • Public username (if entered for leaderboard display)

Flick Fu does not require account creation.

2. Leaderboards

Leaderboard entries are publicly visible by username. Users may change or delete their displayed name at any time within the app.

If you would like your leaderboard records deleted, you may request deletion by emailing us at the contact address above.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain leaderboards
  • Improve typing performance tracking
  • Fix bugs and monitor app stability
  • Display advertisements
  • Process in-app purchases

4. Third-Party Services

Flick Fu uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Cloud Platform / Firestore (data storage)
  • Sentry (error reporting)
  • RevenueCat (in-app purchase management)

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your Advertising ID to provide personalized ads. You can reset or disable your Advertising ID in your device settings.

6. In-App Purchases

Flick Fu offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove ads. All payments are processed securely through Google Play. We do not collect or store payment information.

7. Data Storage

Leaderboard and performance data are stored on Google Cloud Firestore servers located in the United States.

8. Data Retention

We retain leaderboard records unless a deletion request is submitted. Crash and analytics data may be retained according to third-party service policies.

9. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your leaderboard data by contacting us at the email address provided above.

10. Children's Privacy

Flick Fu is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
